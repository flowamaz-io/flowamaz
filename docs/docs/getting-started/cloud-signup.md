# Sign Up for Flowamaz Cloud

Flowamaz Cloud is the fully managed version. We host and operate everything; you just
build workflows. Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial.

## Create your account

1. Go to [app.flowamaz.io](https://app.flowamaz.io) and click **Start free trial**.
2. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Organisation name** — your company or team name.
   - **Organisation URL** — a short slug used in your address, e.g. `acme` becomes
     `acme.flowamaz.io`. Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only.
   - **Your email** — this becomes the first OrgOwner login.
   - **Password** — at least 12 characters.
3. **Pick a plan.** Starter, Pro, or Enterprise. You can change this any time during or
   after the trial. See [Plans comparison](plans-comparison) for the full breakdown.
4. **Complete the onboarding wizard.** It walks you through naming your first workspace
   and choosing how you'll create your first workflow.
5. **Create your first workspace.** Workspaces isolate teams and projects. Most people
   start with one and add more later.

## What the 14-day trial includes

- Full access to every feature on your chosen plan — nothing is locked behind a paywall
  during the trial.
- All AI providers, team invitations, and SSO setup.
- No credit card required to start.

## What happens after the trial

- Near the end of the trial we email a reminder.
- Add a payment method before day 14 to continue without interruption.
- If you don't, the account moves to a read-only state: your workflows and run history
  are preserved, but new runs pause until you upgrade. Nothing is deleted.

To continue, open **Settings → Billing** and add a card. See the
[Upgrade guide](../billing/upgrade-guide) for what changes the moment you upgrade.
