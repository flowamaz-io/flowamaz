# Invite Team Members

Workspaces on Cloud plans are collaborative. Add the people you work with and give each
the right role.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Click **Invite**.
3. Enter the person's **email address**.
4. Pick a **role**: Admin, Designer, Operator, Runner, or Viewer. See
   [Roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions) for what each can do.
5. Confirm. They appear in the member list immediately.

## If the email isn't found

In Phase 1, Flowamaz does not send email invitations. The person you're inviting must
already have a Flowamaz account in your organisation. If their email isn't recognised:

1. Ask them to register at [app.flowamaz.io](https://app.flowamaz.io) first (or have an OrgOwner add them
   to the organisation).
2. Once their account exists, return to **Settings → Members** and invite them again.

This is why the invite step matches on an existing account rather than emailing a link.

## Change someone's role

1. In **Settings → Members**, find the person.
2. Open their role dropdown and choose a new role.
3. The change applies right away — their next action uses the new permissions.

## Remove a member

1. In **Settings → Members**, find the person.
2. Click **Remove**.
3. They lose access to this workspace immediately. Their past actions remain in the
   audit log; nothing they created is deleted.

## What "inactive" means

A member shows as **inactive** when their account exists but they haven't signed in or
accepted access to this workspace yet. Inactive members don't count toward your active
member billing until they actually use the workspace.

You need the **Admin** role (or OrgOwner) to manage members.
