# Community vs Cloud

Both editions run the same workflow engine. The difference is who hosts it and what's
included around it.

## What Community includes

Community Edition is free, self-hosted, and genuinely useful — it is not a crippled
demo. You get:

- The **full workflow engine** with durable execution and exactly-once guarantees.
- **5 workflows** and **500 runs per month**.
- **1 workspace** and **1 user**.
- **7-day run history**.
- The complete `fmz` CLI, the visual canvas, Git-native versioning, dry-run and
  debugger, community connectors, and webhook triggers.
- **Claude Haiku via your own API key (BYOK)** for AI features.

Community is the right choice for individual developers, evaluation, and small personal
automations.

## What Cloud adds

Cloud is the managed edition. On top of everything Community has, you get:

- **Teams** — multiple members per workspace with roles and permissions.
- **More capacity** — up to unlimited workflows, far higher run limits, and more
  workspaces (see [Plans comparison](../getting-started/plans-comparison)).
- **All AI providers** — Anthropic, Azure OpenAI, Google, and more, not just Claude
  Haiku.
- **SSO** — SAML and SCIM for organisation-wide sign-on.
- **Support** — direct help from the Flowamaz team.
- **Managed infrastructure** — we run, patch, and scale everything.

## An honest comparison

Community isn't a trap to pull you onto Cloud. If you're one developer automating your
own work, Community may be all you ever need. Move to Cloud when you genuinely need a
team, more capacity, more AI providers, or SSO — not before.

When you're ready, the move is one command and your work comes with you. See
[Migrate Community to Cloud](../getting-started/migrate-community-to-cloud).
