# Upgrade Guide

Upgrading moves you to a higher plan with bigger limits and more features. It takes
effect immediately.

## How to upgrade

1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Upgrade**.
3. Choose your new plan — Starter, Pro, or Enterprise. See
   [Plans comparison](../getting-started/plans-comparison) for what each includes.
4. Add or confirm your payment method.
5. Confirm. The new plan is active right away.

## What happens immediately

- **Limits increase** — your workflow, run, workspace, and member caps jump to the new
  plan's values the moment the upgrade completes.
- **Features unlock** — additional AI providers, more workspaces, SSO, and support
  become available instantly.
- Work already in progress keeps running; nothing is interrupted.

## Proration, explained plainly

When you upgrade mid-cycle, you only pay the difference for the days remaining in the
current billing period. For example, if you upgrade halfway through the month, you're
charged about half the price difference between the old and new plan for that month.
From the next cycle, you pay the full new plan price. You're never double-charged for
the time you've already paid.

## Payment methods

Flowamaz Cloud accepts **credit and debit cards**. You can update your card any time in
**Settings → Billing**.

## If a payment fails

We don't cut you off the moment a charge fails:

1. **We retry** the payment and email you.
2. **Grace period** — your account keeps working normally while you fix the card.
3. **Suspension** — if payment still hasn't gone through after the grace period, new
   runs pause. Your workflows, history, and data are preserved, not deleted. Update your
   card to resume immediately.

To downgrade or cancel, use the same **Settings → Billing** screen. For invoice details,
see [Understanding your invoice](understanding-your-invoice).
