# Billing

Flowamaz Cloud bills per workspace through Stripe. This article covers the plans, the
free trial, upgrading, and finding your invoices.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | For |
|------|-------|-----|
| **Community** | Free, self-hosted | Individual developers and evaluation. |
| **Starter** | $49/mo | Small teams getting workflows into production. |
| **Pro** | $149/mo | Growing teams that need higher limits and SSO. |

Each plan raises run limits and unlocks features. Community runs on your own
infrastructure; Starter and Pro are managed Cloud plans.

## Free trial

New Cloud workspaces start with a **free trial** so you can build and run real
workflows before paying. During the trial you have full access to your plan's
features. When it ends, add a payment method to keep running production instances —
your workflows and history are preserved.

## Upgrading

1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Upgrade** and choose Starter or Pro.
3. Complete **Stripe checkout** with your card details.
4. The new limits apply immediately.

You can change plans at any time. When you hit a run limit, Flowamaz shows an upgrade
prompt that links straight here.

## Invoices and payment

Billing is handled by Stripe. Open the **Stripe customer portal** from
**Settings → Billing** to:

- Download past **invoices** and receipts.
- Update your card or billing email.
- View upcoming charges.
- Cancel or change your subscription.

## Run limits

Production instances count against your plan's monthly run limit. **Test runs do not
count** (and expire after 24h), so you can iterate freely while building. If you're
close to a limit, the billing page shows current usage so you can upgrade before runs
are blocked.
